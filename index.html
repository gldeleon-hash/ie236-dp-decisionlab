Understand the recursion

At the last shift, calculate what it costs to finish from each possible battery level. Then solve Shift 9 using the best costs already calculated for Shift 10. Repeat back to Shift 1. The solver records both the lowest remaining cost and the charging action producing it.

What if tomorrow's workload is uncertain?

In the 10-shift game, demand is known. Now explore a separate, two-shift scenario based on Shifts 9 and 10. You begin Shift 9 with 1 battery unit—regardless of the battery level you reached in your earlier game. You must choose how much to charge before you learn the workload.

Part B · Play the uncertain scenario

Commit now. Discover the demand afterward.
